Half-day leave can be availed for the first half or second half of a working day. Half-day leave is deducted as 0.5 days from the applicable leave balance.

Buddies help with cultural integration, process navigation, relationship building, and practical questions. Buddy program participation is recognized as a development activity.

The company maintains formal service level agreements for internal HR services including recruitment turnaround times, query resolution timelines, policy clarification response times, and document processing durations. SLA performance is reported monthly and improvement actions are taken for breaches.

The company provides comprehensive tax planning workshops in January and February each year helping employees understand available deductions, exemptions, and investment options for tax savings. The payroll team is available for individual consultations regarding tax-efficient salary structuring.

The company operates a formal alumni rehire program welcoming back former employees who left in good standing. Rehired alumni may receive accelerated onboarding, tenure credit for eligible benefits, and recognition of their previous institutional knowledge. The rehire process includes standard background verification.

The company maintains detailed workforce analytics dashboards tracking diversity metrics, attrition patterns, hiring effectiveness, engagement trends, and compensation equity. Analytics insights inform strategic HR decisions and are shared with leadership monthly. Data privacy is maintained in all analytics reporting.

The company provides comprehensive bereavement support beyond leave including access to grief counseling, flexible return-to-work arrangements, and manager guidance on supporting bereaved team members. The EAP extends bereavement support to family members of the affected employee.

The company operates a formal talent exchange program with other organizations in the industry consortium. Exchange programs allow employees to gain experience in different organizational contexts while maintaining their employment with the company. Exchanges typically last 3 to 6 months.

The company maintains an up-to-date organization chart accessible to all employees through the company intranet. The chart displays reporting relationships, team structures, and contact information. Changes to organizational structure are reflected within 5 working days of effective date.

The company provides comprehensive onboarding support for lateral hires at senior levels including executive coaching for the first 6 months, stakeholder mapping, and strategic context briefings. Senior hire assimilation is tracked through 30-60-90 day check-ins with HR.

The company operates a formal continuous listening strategy combining annual engagement surveys, quarterly pulse checks, always-on feedback channels, and AI-powered sentiment analysis of internal communications. Insights are synthesized into actionable recommendations for leadership.

The company ensures all people policies are written in clear and accessible language avoiding unnecessary jargon or complex legal terminology. Policies are available in multiple languages for global operations. Simplified policy summaries are provided alongside detailed policy documents for easy reference.
